# 🚗 Car Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is a complete data analysis project based on car sales data.

The goal of the project is to explore the dataset, clean the data, analyze sales and revenue performance, identify important business patterns, and present the results using data visualizations and business insights.

The project was developed using Python and Pandas, with Matplotlib and Seaborn for data visualization.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Explore and understand the car sales dataset
* Identify missing values and duplicate records
* Clean and prepare the data for analysis
* Analyze sales performance by brand, model, city, and salesperson
* Analyze revenue performance
* Identify the most frequently used payment method
* Analyze monthly revenue patterns
* Calculate important averages
* Create data visualizations
* Extract meaningful business insights from the analysis

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains car sales transactions with information about:

* Sale ID
* Date
* Brand
* Model
* Year
* Price
* Quantity
* City
* Salesperson
* Payment Method
* Customer Age
* Revenue

The `Revenue` value is based on the car price and quantity sold.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis produced several important findings:

* **Mercedes** was the best-selling brand, with **253 cars sold**.
* The **Mercedes GLC** generated the highest revenue among the models, with approximately **5.77 million** in total revenue.
* **Madaba** recorded the highest sales volume, with **254 cars sold**.
* **Noor** generated the highest total revenue among the salespeople, with approximately **8.29 million**.
* **Credit Card** was the most frequently used payment method, with **282 transactions**.
* The average car price was approximately **37,385**.
* **December** recorded the highest total revenue among all months.
* **Credit Card** generated the highest total revenue among the payment methods.
